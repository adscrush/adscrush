---
title: "Media Buyers"
description: "Manage media buyer (affiliate) relationships and access"
---

Media buyers (affiliates/publishers) are the partners who drive traffic. They manage ad accounts and earn payouts on conversions.

## Media Buyer Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Name** | Media buyer name |
| **Email** | Contact email |
| **Status** | Active, Pending, Suspended |
| **Kind** | Internal (employee), External (third-party affiliate) |
| **Employee Link** | For internal media buyers, link to employee account |

## Kinds

| Kind | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `internal` | An employee who also runs traffic campaigns |
| `external` | A third-party affiliate partner |

## Product Visibility

Media buyers can only promote products they have access to, based on product visibility settings:

- **Public products** — Available to all active media buyers
- **Private products** — Requires explicit approval
- **Exclusive products** — Invitation only

## Bulk Operations

- Bulk delete
- Bulk status update (activate, suspend)
- Link to employee accounts

## Ad Account Ownership

Each media buyer owns their ad accounts (Facebook Business Manager, Google Ads accounts, etc.). These ad accounts are assigned to campaigns for tracking link generation.
