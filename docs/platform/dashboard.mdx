The dashboard provides a high-level overview of your entire operation with real-time KPIs and visualizations.

## KPI Cards

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Clicks | Total clicks across all campaigns |
| Conversions | Total conversions |
| Conversion Rate | (Conversions / Clicks) × 100 |
| Payout | Total payout to media buyers |
| Revenue | Total revenue from advertisers |
| Profit | Revenue minus payout |
| RPC | Revenue per click |
| EPC | Earnings per click |

Each KPI shows a comparison with the previous period (percentage change).

## Period Selector

Filter the dashboard by:

- Today
- Yesterday
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 3 months
- Last 12 months
- Custom date range

## Geography Panel

Map visualization showing click and conversion distribution by country.

## Active Offers

Quick overview of your top-performing active products/offers.

## Customer Venn

Visual representation of data relationships across your campaigns.
